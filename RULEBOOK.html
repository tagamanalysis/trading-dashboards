EQHL v2 — Complete Trading Rulebook

All 5 locked, fully backtested configurations · 2021–2025

Core Mechanism (identical across all pairs)

Setup name: EQH/EQL Liquidity Sweep → iFVG → CHoCH

1Swing Detection

A confirmed swing high/low uses a 2-bar-left, 2-bar-right pivot (candle must be the highest/lowest of the 5-candle window centered on it). Confirmed 2 bars after formation.

2EQH/EQL Pool Formation

Swing highs (or lows) within a price tolerance cluster into a "liquidity pool." Matching is pure tolerance-based — no slope or directional restriction (tested and rejected, see below). Pool level = running weighted average of all touches. Active once minimum touch count reached.

Tolerance: 4 pips   Min touches: 2   Pool lookback: 300 bars

3Sweep Detection

A pool is "swept" when a candle's wick exceeds the pool level but the candle's close comes back inside it. Sweeping an EQH → bearish/short bias. Sweeping an EQL → bullish/long bias.

4FVG Formation (local to each sweep)

After a sweep, scan for a 3-candle Fair Value Gap forming opposite the sweep bias, within a 20-bar window. Bullish FVG: low[now] > high[2 bars ago]. Bearish FVG: high[now] < low[2 bars ago]. No minimum gap size in the core validated model.

5FVG State Machine

0 = Formed  →  1 = Retraced (price wicks back in)  →  2 = Inverted/Armed (price closes beyond far boundary). Must invert within 30 bars of forming, then stays armed for another 30 bars.

6CHoCH — local / sweep-relative

Only swing pivots forming after the sweep bar are tracked (reset per sweep — no dependency on any prior global trend state). CHoCH confirms the moment price closes beyond the most recent such pivot, opposite the sweep direction.

7Entry

The CHoCH confirmation candle is the entry, provided a state-2 (armed) FVG of the correct direction already exists from this same sweep.

8Stop-Loss

Wick extreme of the last 10 bars, plus a 0.2 pip buffer.

9Structure-Size Filter

Stop distance must fall between 1 pip minimum and a per-instrument maximum (see table below). Trades outside this range are skipped.

10Take-Profit

Fixed R-multiple from entry, per-instrument RR value. One trade at a time — no new entries while a position is open.

Day-Type Classification (used for EURUSD Prop Firm and XAUUSD only)

Per session (Asian 00–08 UTC, London 08–16 UTC, NY 16–24 UTC), compute:

Efficiency Ratio (ER) = |close−open| / Σ|high−low| per bar
Range = session high − session low

Compare both against the trailing median of the last 20 occurrences of that same session type (Asian vs Asian, London vs London, NY vs NY — never mixed). Classified using the prior completed session's realized values — no lookahead.

LabelCondition
Trend-VolatileER ≥ median AND Range ≥ median
Trend-CalmER ≥ median AND Range < median
Range-Volatile (Choppy)ER < median AND Range ≥ median
Range-Calm (Dead)ER < median AND Range < median

For EURUSD, Trend-Volatile was the standout bucket everywhere — London × Trend-Volatile = +0.174R, Sharpe 4.00, the strongest single cell in the whole dataset.

Highest Sharpe of any pair in the whole project. Structure-size ceiling was the critical fix -- default 20bps gave Sharpe 2.06, DD -35.7%; widening to 80bps (same lesson as GBPJPY) gave Sharpe 5.85 before the day filter. Saturday is the one clean, decisive exclusion: lowest volatility day of the week, Sharpe -0.13 alone, worst DD of any day (-30.4%). Excluding it improved every metric simultaneously (Sharpe 5.85→6.54, DD unchanged, 82% trade retention) -- a genuine free win, not a tradeoff. RR=1.0 was tested at the same struct ceiling and lost on Sharpe (5.57) and DD (-25.6%) despite similar expectancy -- RR=0.75 confirmed correct. IS/OOS holds up well (Sharpe 4.03 IS → 5.02 OOS, expectancy improves out-of-sample).

Market profile: BTCUSD is structurally very different from any forex pair -- ~42 raw sweeps/day, 99.5% produce a gap, 89.8% of those invert. Almost none of forex's natural upstream filtering exists; CHoCH + the day-of-week filter are doing most of the real selectivity. 2021-2025 covers a +57.6% bull year, a -65.3% crash, a +154.5% recovery, and more -- the edge held up across genuinely different market regimes.

Key Cross-Pair Finding

EURUSD wants volatility. GBPJPY and XAUUSD want calm. Trend-Volatile is EURUSD's best bucket everywhere, but is GBPJPY's weakest bucket and XAUUSD's worst-drawdown bucket. This is a genuine, opposite-direction, replicated finding — not noise. No universal day-type rule applies across all pairs; each was tuned individually.

Rejected / Tested-and-Discarded Ideas

Kept here so they are never accidentally re-tried.

Asymmetric slope rule on EQH/EQL pool matching (EQH flat/≤1p up or any down, EQL mirrored) — looked visually sensible, cut GBPUSD's Sharpe by ~43% and expectancy by ~42% in a real backtest. Do not use.
Minimum FVG gap size (0.3p) — mild net positive in isolation (Sharpe 6.47→6.69 on GBPUSD) but not part of the core locked model.
CHoCH previous-close-exclusion rule — mild net negative in isolation (Sharpe 6.47→6.03). Not part of core model.
Prior-same-session-direction filter — real, replicated per-bucket pattern across all 4 pairs, but LOST Sharpe when actually applied (cuts ~50% of trades for worse Sharpe on every pair).
PDH/PDL location filter — looked strong on GBPUSD alone, did not replicate (XAUUSD contradicted it). Rejected.
Calendar-month seasonality — no month clears significance, EXCEPT October (real, cross-pair-replicated edge, all 4 pairs agree Oct > Dec). Documented, not yet acted on.
iFVG size quality filter — strongest survivor of the full Families A–L sweep, replicates directionally, clears the 70–80% retention bar — but Sharpe impact is roughly neutral to mildly negative on 3 of 4 pairs. Marginal, not adopted.
