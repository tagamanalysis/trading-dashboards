Trading SOP — Live Execution & Risk Management

Companion to the Rulebook — this is HOW you trade, the Rulebook is WHAT you trade

1. Risk Per Trade

Standard: 0.5% of current account balance per trade

Confirmed via backtest (Sept 2026): at 0.5% risk, every tested pair combination — solo and pooled — stayed safely within a real $12,000 max-loss limit on a $200,000 funded account (6% rule), with real margin (best case −3.48% DD, worst case −4.64% DD, all well under the 6% ceiling). At 1% risk, individual pairs alone would have breached the limit historically.

Never deviate from 0.5% without re-running the loss-limit check first. If account size or loss-limit % changes, the safe risk-per-trade number changes too.

These are not arbitrary — each was tested against the alternative (1.0 vs 0.75) and the value above won on both Sharpe and drawdown for that specific pair.

Discretionary exit management is allowed and is NOT a system violation. Moving SL to breakeven, trailing based on live structure, or taking partial profit beyond the fixed target is a skill layer the backtest cannot model — it was never coded, so it was never tested.

This means your discretionary exits have no historical track record yet. Log every discretionary exit separately (fixed-target R vs actual-exit R vs max structurally-available R) so a real track record builds over time, rather than assuming skill without evidence.

5. When Not To Trade

  • Any pair during its excluded day-type (XAUUSD + Trend-Volatile) or excluded day (BTCUSD + Saturday)
  • Within 15 minutes of high-impact GBP/USD news (GBPUSD, EURUSD only)
  • If you notice yourself modifying a rule "just this once" — stop and write down WHY, don't silently deviate

6. Pair Priority By Session

(Informational — not a hard rule.) London session is strongest for every forex/gold pair (GBPUSD, EURUSD, GBPJPY, XAUUSD). NY session is strongest for BTCUSD specifically — the one pair where this pattern reverses. Asian session is universally the weakest, though still positive on every pair. Your actual desk hours (3:30–9:30PM IST) already overlap heavily with London — no schedule change needed.

7. Rule Deviation Log

Any time you trade outside the rules above (different RR, ignored a day-type exclusion, traded during excluded hours, etc.) — write it down immediately in the journal with the reason. This is not about guilt, it's about being able to later separate "the system underperformed" from "I deviated from the system." Undocumented deviations make it impossible to tell these apart after the fact.
